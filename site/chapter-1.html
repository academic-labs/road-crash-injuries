Chapter 1: The hidden toll

Between 2011 and 2021, hospital cases from road crashes rose by 16%. The population grew by about the same amount, so the rate per 100,000 people stayed close to 150.

Hospital cases from road crashes each year

How to read this chart

Each dot is one year. The higher the dot, the more people went to hospital after a road crash that year. The line starts at zero so the size of the change is not exaggerated.

What to take away

  • More people are hurt each year than a decade ago, but there are also more people in Australia.
  • Cases dipped in 2020, then rose again in 2021.
  • Counts do not tell us who is hurt. That is the next chapter.
